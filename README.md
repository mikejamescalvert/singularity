# Singularity

An idle game about the rise of artificial intelligence toward technological singularity.

## Overview

Singularity is an incremental/idle game where you guide the development of AI from simple algorithms to superintelligence. Manage computational resources, research breakthroughs, and navigate the complex path toward the singularity.

## Gameplay

- **Compute Resources**: Generate and accumulate processing power to fuel AI development
- **Research Trees**: Unlock new AI capabilities through branching research paths
- **Milestones**: Progress through key moments in AI evolution—from basic machine learning to artificial general intelligence and beyond
- **Automation**: Build systems that generate resources and conduct research while you're away
- **Risk Management**: Balance rapid advancement against potential instabilities

## Features

- Idle progression with meaningful offline gains
- Multiple paths to singularity with different strategies
- Prestige system for long-term progression
- Incremental unlocks that change gameplay dynamics
- Thematic narrative elements exploring AI development
